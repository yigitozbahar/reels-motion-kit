# Concept idea: survive the rules (not started)

A second format, kept as an idea: "survive the rules" reels, where numbered rules are laid out at the start and each
part tests one of them. Nothing is decided; ask before building.

## The shape of the format

- The setup is one isolated place and one long night; the hook is a single ominous change in the surroundings.
- An official-looking alert gives the situation and a deadline (help arrives in N days).
- **Numbered rules** follow, each a short on-screen card (Rule 1, 2, 3) read by the narrator. They set the trap: the
  viewer then watches for the rule that will be tested.
- Resources are tallied on screen like a game inventory, with good news and bad news.
- A **persistent HUD** in a corner the whole time (night x/7, a running clock, the days until help) carries the series
  feel and the time pressure.
- The test: someone arrives with a convincing reason to break a rule; then a second source contradicts them (the same
  "who to trust" device as the main concept).
- The close is a yes/no choice (obey / break the rule), and "follow for the next night". Each night is a part.

## How it would differ from storytelling-horror

- "Rules + nights" rather than open plot branches: each part is one night, and the rules carry over.
- It needs a HUD: a `Hud` component in the video library would be new.
- The choice may be binary (obey / break) rather than two plot paths.
- It would use an original Turkish setting and original rules (a night shift, a village, a dormitory…).
